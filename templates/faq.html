Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about reporting, finding, and safely retrieving belongings on campus.

Log in to FindMate using your student or staff account and click "Report an Item". Select "I Lost an Item", enter details like brand, color, location where it was last seen, and upload a reference photo if available. When someone turns in a matching item, you will be notified.

Click "Report an Item" and choose "I Found an Item". Take a clear photo, describe the building or hall where you discovered it, and indicate whether you turned it in to a campus desk (such as the Hamilton Library Hub or Campus Center Information Counter) or are holding it safely.

To prevent fraudulent claims, anyone claiming an item must submit proof of ownership through our secure verification form. This can include describing a hidden sticker, lockscreen wallpaper, serial number, scratch, or entering the device passcode in person during handoff. The reporter or campus desk reviews the proof before approving the handover.

For student safety, all in-person exchanges are recommended to take place at designated Campus Safe Zones:
  • Hamilton Central Library Hub — Main Security Desk (Ground Floor)
  • Campus Center Information Desk — Room 102
  • Public Safety Building — Front Reception (24/7)
